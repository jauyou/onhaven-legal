# Onhaven Support

Onhaven is a private personal and family finance app for iPhone. It brings your investments, income, spending, and goals together, and evaluates them every quarter. All of this runs on your device. For a walkthrough of every feature, see the [User Guide](/onhaven-legal/guide/).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Getting started

**Do I need an account?**

No. Every feature works without signing in. Sign in with Apple is only used for family sync.

**Can I try it before entering my own data?**

Yes. Tap **Explore with Sample Data** during setup, or go to **Evaluate → Settings (gear icon) → Sample Data**. While sample data is loaded, sync and statement import are paused. **Clear Sample Data** removes only the sample records.

### Statements and data

**Which statements can I import?**

- **Brokerage statements**, as PDF or image, on the Portfolio tab
- **Credit card statements**, on the Cash Flow tab → Cards
- **Bank statements**, as PDF, on the Cash Flow tab → Bank

Brokerage and card statements need Apple Intelligence. Bank statements must be the PDF your bank provides, because scans have no text layer. You can always enter records by hand.

**"Statement reading isn't available on this device."**

Brokerage and card statement reading needs an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on in *Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri*. If it says the model is still being prepared, the model is downloading. Keep the phone on Wi-Fi and charging, then try again later.

**A statement was read incorrectly.**

Open **Imported Statements** (on the Portfolio tab, or in the Cards or Bank section) and delete that statement. Everything it imported is removed, and you can import it again. If it keeps happening with a particular bank or broker, please email us the institution's name and a description of what went wrong. Please don't attach the statement itself.

**Does Onhaven connect to my bank?**

No. Onhaven never asks for bank or brokerage logins. You import statements yourself.

### Family sync

**How does family sync keep my data private?**

Records are encrypted on your device with a family key before they're uploaded. The key passes between family devices by QR code and never reaches our server, so we can't read your records.

**I got a new phone and the app says the family key isn't on this device.**

Either use **Help Recover Key** on another family member's device and scan its QR code, or enter your recovery key (it starts with `OHVKEY1-`). See [Family sync](/onhaven-legal/guide/#8-family-sync) in the User Guide.

**I lost every family device and my recovery key.**

We're sorry, but we can't recover your records. We never have the key that encrypts them. Your account itself still works, and you can create a new family.

**Why is sync paused?**

Sync is paused while sample data is loaded, so the sample records aren't shared with your family. Clear the sample data first.

### Account and privacy

**How do I delete my account?**

In the app, open the last tab (**Account** or **Family**) and scroll to the bottom, or open **Family Sync**, then tap **Delete Account** and confirm with Face ID and Apple. Your account, its devices, and its invites are deleted from our server immediately. If you own a family that has other members, you'll first choose to transfer ownership or delete the family. Records on your device are kept. To remove them too, delete the app.

If you can't access the app, email us from the address tied to your Apple ID (or your Apple private relay address), and we will delete your account within 30 days.

**What data leaves my phone?**

See the [Privacy Policy](/onhaven-legal/). In short:

- Ticker symbols, to fetch prices
- Your crypto wallet's public address, if you add a wallet
- Encrypted records, if you use family sync
- Statement pages or figures sent to Apple's Private Cloud Compute, when that model is used; the app tells you each time

**Is the analysis financial advice?**

No. Onhaven's evaluations, recommendations, and AI analysis are for information only. Please consult a qualified professional before making financial decisions.

### Crypto

**Can Onhaven move my crypto?**

No. Wallet tracking is read-only. You only give Onhaven a public address. It never asks for a seed phrase or private key.
